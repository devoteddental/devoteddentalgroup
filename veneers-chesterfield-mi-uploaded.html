Our Devoted team answers the most common questions about dental veneers in Chesterfield, MI.

What Are Dental Veneers?

Veneers are ultra-thin shells of porcelain or composite resin that are bonded to the front surface of your teeth. They're custom-designed to match the color, shape, and size that best suits your face, creating a natural, beautiful result that looks like your real teeth, only better. Veneers can address discoloration that doesn't respond to whitening, chips and cracks, uneven or worn-down teeth, gaps between teeth, and misshapen teeth.

Porcelain vs. Composite Veneers

Porcelain veneers offer the most natural appearance and the longest lifespan, typically lasting 15 to 20 years with proper care. Composite veneers are applied directly to the tooth in a single visit, are more affordable, and can be repaired if chipped — though they typically last 5 to 7 years. Our Chesterfield team will help you choose the right type based on your goals and budget.

The Veneer Process at Devoted Dental Chesterfield

Getting veneers at Devoted Dental Chesterfield starts with a consultation to discuss your smile goals and determine whether veneers are right for you. If so, a small amount of enamel is removed from the tooth surface, impressions are taken, and temporary veneers are placed while your permanent ones are crafted. At your second visit, your custom veneers are bonded permanently in place.

FAQ

Common Questions About
Dental Veneers

1How much do veneers cost in Chesterfield, MI?

The cost of veneers depends on the type (porcelain vs. composite) and how many teeth are being treated. Devoted Dental Chesterfield offers financing through CareCredit and Cherry Financing to make treatment accessible. Call for a personalized estimate.

2How long do dental veneers last?

Porcelain veneers typically last 15 to 20 years. Composite veneers last 5 to 7 years. With proper care, including regular checkups and avoiding biting hard objects. Your veneers can look great for decades.

3Are veneers permanent?

Porcelain veneers require a small amount of enamel removal, making the process irreversible. It's important to be certain about your decision before proceeding. Our Chesterfield team will walk you through all your options at your consultation.

4Do veneers look natural?

Yes. Custom porcelain veneers are designed to match your surrounding teeth in color, shape, and translucency. Most people won't be able to tell the difference between veneers and natural teeth.
