Also accepted

We Also Accept CareCredit

CareCredit is a healthcare credit card that offers promotional 0% interest financing on qualifying purchases of $200 or more, for patients who prefer a revolving credit card over an installment plan.

Promotional 0% interest for 6, 12, 18, or 24 months on qualifying purchases
Longer-term reduced-APR plans available for larger treatment amounts
Usable at hundreds of thousands of healthcare providers nationwide, not just Devoted Dental
Important: this is deferred interest, not true 0% APR — if the full balance isn't paid off within the promotional period, interest is charged retroactively on the entire original purchase amount from the purchase date
Learn More at carecredit.com
