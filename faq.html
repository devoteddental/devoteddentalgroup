New Patients & Appointments

Yes. All 5 of our Metro Detroit offices — Chesterfield, Highland, Livonia, Warren, and Wyandotte — are always accepting new patients, and most locations offer same-day availability.
Please bring a photo ID, your insurance card (if you have one), and a list of any medications you're currently taking. If you're a new patient, arriving 10–15 minutes early to complete paperwork helps keep your visit on schedule.
In many cases, yes. Same-day availability is one of the reasons patients choose Devoted Dental for both routine care and dental emergencies. Call your nearest office directly to check same-day openings.
Hours vary by office, with several locations opening as early as 8:00 AM and staying open into the evening on select days. Check the specific hours for your nearest location on our Locations page.
Just call your office as soon as you know you need to reschedule. We understand plans change, and our team will help find a new time that works for you.
While appointments are recommended so we can prepare for your visit, our offices do their best to accommodate walk-ins, especially for dental emergencies. Calling ahead helps us get you seen faster.
Yes, all 5 Devoted Dental Group locations across Macomb, Oakland, and Wayne counties offer on-site patient parking.

Insurance, Medicaid & Financing

Yes, Medicaid is accepted at all 5 of our Metro Detroit locations. Call your nearest office to verify your specific plan and coverage details.
We accept Medicaid, Medicare, and 50+ PPO insurance plans across all locations. Call your nearest office to verify your specific coverage.
Coverage varies by plan type and can change, so the most reliable way to confirm is to call your nearest office directly and we'll verify your specific coverage before your visit.
You're still welcome at any of our 5 offices. Between our Cherry and CareCredit financing options and transparent treatment pricing, we work to make care accessible whether or not you're insured.
Cherry is a flexible payment plan with 0% APR options for qualified patients. It uses a soft credit check, so applying doesn't impact your credit score, and approval typically takes under 60 seconds — even during an emergency visit.
Cherry is an installment payment plan with a soft credit check and true 0% APR options. CareCredit is a healthcare credit card with a hard credit check that offers promotional 0% interest periods, though interest can be charged retroactively if the balance isn't paid off in time. We accept both, so you can choose whichever fits your situation.
Applying through Cherry uses a soft credit check and won't affect your score. CareCredit requires a hard credit check, which can have a small, temporary impact.
Yes. Larger treatment plans, including dental implants, oral surgery, and Invisalign, are commonly financed through Cherry or CareCredit so you can spread the cost into manageable monthly payments.

Our 5 Metro Detroit Locations

We have 5 locations across Metro Detroit: Chesterfield and Warren in Macomb County, Highland in Oakland County, and Livonia and Wyandotte in Wayne County. Visit our Locations page to find addresses, hours, and directions for the office nearest you.
Hours vary slightly by location, with most offices open Monday through Friday and select Saturday availability once a month. You can see the exact hours for each of our 5 offices on our Locations page.
Our core services, including cleanings, exams, fillings, crowns, and emergency care, are available at every location. Some specialized treatments may vary slightly by office, so call ahead if you're looking for a specific procedure.
You're welcome to visit whichever of our 5 Metro Detroit offices is most convenient for you. Many of our patients see us at the location closest to their home or workplace, and switching between offices is easy.
Yes, accessibility is a priority at all of our locations. If you have specific accessibility needs, call ahead and our team will make sure you're comfortable during your visit.
We serve patients throughout Metro Detroit, including Macomb County (Chesterfield, Warren, and New Baltimore), Oakland County (Highland and surrounding communities), and Wayne County (Livonia and Wyandotte).

Emergency Dental Care

Severe or worsening tooth pain, a knocked-out or loose tooth, visible facial or gum swelling, and a crown or filling that's come off exposing sensitive tooth structure are all situations we treat same-day. If you're unsure, call — our team can help you figure out if it's urgent.
In most cases, yes. Same-day emergency appointments are available at all 5 of our Metro Detroit offices for issues like severe tooth pain, swelling, or a broken tooth.
Time matters. Handle the tooth by the crown (not the root), rinse it gently if dirty, and try to place it back in the socket or keep it in milk. Call your nearest Devoted Dental office immediately — getting seen quickly gives the tooth the best chance of being saved.
Weekend availability varies by location, with several offices open one Saturday a month. Call your nearest office to confirm current emergency availability, or call ahead so our team can advise on next steps.
Cost depends on the treatment needed, but between Medicaid, Medicare, 50+ PPO plans, and Cherry financing with under-60-second approval, most patients are able to get emergency care started the same day regardless of insurance status.

Preventive & General Dentistry

Most patients benefit from a cleaning and checkup every six months. If you have gum disease or other risk factors, your dentist may recommend more frequent visits.
Yes, our offices use modern digital imaging, which uses less radiation than traditional film X-rays and lets your dentist review results immediately.
A typical checkup includes a professional cleaning, a full exam, digital X-rays as needed, and a conversation with your dentist about any concerns or recommended treatment.
Yes, we offer custom-fitted night guards to protect your teeth from the effects of nighttime grinding and clenching.
The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recommends a child's first dental visit by their first birthday, or within six months of their first tooth coming in. Our family dentists at every location are experienced with young patients.
Yes, preventive treatments like fluoride and dental sealants are available for both children and adults as part of our general dentistry services.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Yes, professional teeth whitening performed or supervised by a dentist is safe for most patients. It's more effective and better controlled than over-the-counter kits, and your dentist can advise on the best option for your teeth.
Veneers are thin, custom-made shells (typically porcelain) permanently bonded to the front of your teeth for a long-lasting cosmetic transformation. Dental bonding uses a tooth-colored resin applied directly to the tooth — it's a faster, more affordable option, though generally less durable than veneers.
Invisalign uses a series of clear, removable aligners instead of metal brackets and wires, making it a discreet, comfortable option for straightening teeth. It's often preferred by adults and teens who want a less noticeable treatment without the food restrictions or office visits that traditional braces require. Invisalign is the teeth-straightening option we offer at Devoted Dental — your dentist can walk you through whether you're a good candidate at a consultation.
Treatment time varies by case, but many patients complete Invisalign treatment in 12 to 18 months. Your dentist will give you a personalized timeline after your consultation.
Purely cosmetic treatments like teeth whitening are typically not covered by insurance, though procedures with a functional benefit, like some crowns or bonding, may be partially covered. We accept Cherry and CareCredit financing to help make cosmetic treatment affordable either way.

Restorative Dentistry & Oral Surgery

Implant cost depends on how many teeth are being replaced and whether additional procedures like bone grafting are needed. Our team provides a personalized cost estimate at your consultation, and Cherry and CareCredit financing are both available to spread the cost into monthly payments.
Modern root canals, performed with local anesthesia, are generally no more uncomfortable than getting a filling. Most patients report the actual pain that led them to need the root canal was worse than the procedure itself.
Most patients experience some swelling and soreness for a few days, manageable with rest and over-the-counter or prescribed pain relief. Your dentist will give you specific aftercare instructions, and most people are back to normal activities within a few days to a week.
It depends on your oral health, bone density, and budget. Implants offer a permanent, natural-feeling replacement but require adequate jawbone and a longer treatment timeline. Dentures are a faster, more affordable option and have improved significantly in comfort and fit. Your dentist can walk you through the tradeoffs at a consultation.
Yes, routine extractions and many oral surgery procedures are performed in-house at our offices, meaning fewer referrals and less time between diagnosis and treatment.
A crown is a custom cap placed over a damaged, weakened, or heavily decayed tooth to restore its strength, shape, and appearance. Crowns are commonly recommended after a root canal or when a tooth has a large filling that's at risk of breaking.
A dental bridge is a fixed restoration, permanently attached to the teeth on either side of a gap. A partial denture is removable and clips into place, offering a more affordable option for replacing multiple missing teeth.

Pediatric & Family Dentistry

Yes, family and pediatric dentistry is available at every Devoted Dental Group office, so your whole family can be seen at one convenient location.
Consistent brushing twice a day with a fluoride toothpaste, limiting sugary snacks and drinks (especially juice and sticky candy), and keeping up with regular checkups all go a long way. Baby teeth matter for chewing, speech development, and holding space for adult teeth, so cavities are worth preventing even though those teeth are temporary.
We understand dental anxiety is common in both kids and adults. Call your nearest office to discuss the comfort options available for your specific visit and treatment plan.
Yes, Medicaid is accepted for both children and adults at all 5 of our Metro Detroit locations.
